فُعِلَ

The Passive Verb

الفِعْلُ المَبْنِيُّ لِلْمَجْهُول

المَبْنِيُّ لِلْمَجْهُول
Who Did It? — Never Mind

“A letter was written.” “Fasting has been prescribed.” When the doer is unknown, obvious, or not the point, Arabic changes the verb’s vowels: كَتَبَ ← كُتِبَ, يَكْتُبُ ← يُكْتَبُ. Learn the two shapes, the weak verbs, and see them in the Qur’an.

كَتَبَ — active كُتِبَ — passive
The big idea

مِنَ المَعْلُومِ إِلَى المَجْهُولThree things happen

Active
كَتَبَ زَيْدٌ رِسَالَةً
Zayd wrote a letter
➜
Passive
كُتِبَتْ رِسَالَةٌ
a letter was written
1
The doer disappears. زَيْدٌ is gone — we may not know who wrote it, or it doesn’t matter.
2
The object steps up. رِسَالَةً (accusative) becomes رِسَالَةٌ (nominative) — it is now the deputy of the doer, نَائِبُ الفَاعِل.
3
The verb changes its vowels (and agrees with its new subject: كُتِبَتْ is feminine because رِسَالَة is).

The past tense

صِيغَةُ المَاضِي المَبْنِيِّ لِلْمَجْهُولThree shapes become one: فُعِلَ

The ruleḍamma on the first letter, kasra on the letter before the last — whatever the active verb looked like.

!
A passive needs an object to promote, so only transitive verbs make a real passive. لَعِبَ and ثَقُلَ are intransitive; لُعِبَ بِهِ works only with a preposition, and ثُقِلَ is in your deck just to show the vowel pattern.
From the Qur’an
كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ
Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:183: “Fasting has been prescribed for you, as it was prescribed for those before you.”
لُعِنَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِن بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ عَلَىٰ لِسَانِ دَاوُودَ وَعِيسَى ابْنِ مَرْيَمَ
Sūrah al-Mā’idah, 5:78: “Those who disbelieved among the Children of Israel were cursed by the tongue of David and Jesus, son of Mary.”
وَمَن قُتِلَ مَظْلُومًا فَقَدْ جَعَلْنَا لِوَلِيِّهِ سُلْطَانًا
Sūrah al-Isrā’, 17:33: “Whoever is killed unjustly, We have given his heir authority.”
خُلِقَ مِن مَّاءٍ دَافِقٍ
Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq, 86:6: “He was created from a fluid, ejected.”
وَفُتِحَتِ السَّمَاءُ فَكَانَتْ أَبْوَابًا
Sūrah an-Naba’, 78:19: “and the heaven is opened and becomes gateways.” The verb is feminine to match السَّمَاء.
يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلٌ فَاسْتَمِعُوا لَهُ
Sūrah al-Ḥajj, 22:73: “O people, an example has been presented, so listen to it.”
The present tense

صِيغَةُ المُضَارِعِ المَبْنِيِّ لِلْمَجْهُولThree shapes become one: يُفْعَلُ

The ruleḍamma on the prefix, fatḥa on the letter before the last: يُفْعَلُ.

From the Qur’an
ثُمَّ لَا يُنصَرُونَ
Sūrah Āl ‘Imrān, 3:111: “then they will not be helped.”
يَوْمَ يُكْشَفُ عَن سَاقٍ وَيُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى السُّجُودِ
Sūrah al-Qalam, 68:42: “On the Day the shin is uncovered and they are called to prostrate.” Note يُكْشَفُ عَن سَاقٍ keeps its preposition, like يُلْعَبُ بِقِطٍّ.
وَالَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ لَا يَخْلُقُونَ شَيْئًا وَهُمْ يُخْلَقُونَ
Sūrah an-Naḥl, 16:20: “Those they invoke besides Allah create nothing, while they themselves are created.” Active يَخْلُقُونَ beside passive يُخْلَقُونَ.
وَعَلَيْهَا وَعَلَى الْفُلْكِ تُحْمَلُونَ
Sūrah al-Mu’minūn, 23:22: “and on them and on ships you are carried.”
سَتُكْتَبُ شَهَادَتُهُمْ وَيُسْأَلُونَ
Sūrah az-Zukhruf, 43:19: “Their testimony will be recorded, and they will be questioned.” Feminine تُكْتَبُ for شَهَادَة, like the deck’s تُكْتَبُ رِسَالَةٌ.

From the Qur’an
أُذِنَ لِلَّذِينَ يُقَاتَلُونَ بِأَنَّهُمْ ظُلِمُوا
Sūrah al-Ḥajj, 22:39: “Permission is given to those who are fought, because they have been wronged.” ظُلِمُوا is the past passive of ظَلَمَ (they); يُقَاتَلُونَ is a present passive of a longer verb (see the “Beyond” step).
وَعَلَيْهَا وَعَلَى الْفُلْكِ تُحْمَلُونَ
you (m. pl.), present — Sūrah al-Mu’minūn, 23:22.
وَهُمْ يُخْلَقُونَ
they (m.), present — Sūrah an-Naḥl, 16:20.
وَقَدْ أُخْرِجْنَا مِن دِيَارِنَا وَأَبْنَائِنَا
we, past (from the longer verb أَخْرَجَ) — Sūrah al-Baqarah, 2:246: “we have been driven out of our homes and our children.”

From the Qur’an
وَقِيلَ يَا أَرْضُ ابْلَعِي مَاءَكِ … وَغِيضَ الْمَاءُ وَقُضِيَ الْأَمْرُ
hollow قِيلَ, and the defective قُضِيَ — Sūrah Hūd, 11:44: “And it was said, ‘O earth, swallow your water’… and the matter was decided.”
مَّا يُقَالُ لَكَ إِلَّا مَا قَدْ قِيلَ لِلرُّسُلِ مِن قَبْلِكَ
present and past of قَالَ together — Sūrah Fuṣṣilat, 41:43: “Nothing is said to you except what was already said to the messengers before you.”
وَإِذَا دُعُوا إِلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ لِيَحْكُمَ بَيْنَهُمْ
defective, past plural — Sūrah an-Nūr, 24:48: “And when they are called to Allah and His Messenger so that he may judge between them.”
هَٰذِهِ بِضَاعَتُنَا رُدَّتْ إِلَيْنَا
doubled, past — Sūrah Yūsuf, 12:65: “This is our merchandise; it has been returned to us.”
وَلَا يُرَدُّ بَأْسُهُ عَنِ الْقَوْمِ الْمُجْرِمِينَ
doubled, present — Sūrah al-An‘ām, 6:147: “and His punishment cannot be repelled from the wicked people.”
مَّثَلُ الْجَنَّةِ الَّتِي وُعِدَ الْمُتَّقُونَ
first-weak, past — Sūrah ar-Ra‘d, 13:35: “The description of Paradise, which the righteous have been promised.”
وَفِي السَّمَاءِ رِزْقُكُمْ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ
first-weak, present — Sūrah adh-Dhāriyāt, 51:22: “And in the heaven is your provision and what you are promised.”
i
I have no verse for the past passives of مَدَّ, بَاعَ and رَمَى; the verses use رَدَّ, a doubled verb that behaves the same way as مَدَّ.

Recap

Everything in one place

WhyThe doer is unknown or unimportant: مَبْنِيٌّ لِلْمَجْهُول.
Pastفَعَلَ · فَعِلَ · فَعُلَ ← فُعِلَ — كَتَبَ ← كُتِبَ
Presentيَفْعُلُ · يَفْعَلُ · يَفْعِلُ ← يُفْعَلُ — يَكْتُبُ ← يُكْتَبُ
The objectBecomes the deputy subject, نَائِبُ الفَاعِل, in the nominative; the verb agrees: كُتِبَتْ رِسَالَةٌ.
No objectA preposition phrase takes its place and the verb stays masculine singular: يُلْعَبُ بِقِطٍّ.
Weak & doubledقِيلَ · يُقَالُ · دُعِيَ · يُدْعَى · مُدَّ · يُمَدُّ · وُعِدَ · يُوعَدُ
